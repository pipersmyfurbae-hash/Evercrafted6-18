# Evercrafted Editorial Brand and Commerce Site Copy

**Status:** Approved implementation draft; not yet applied in Wix  
**Scope:** Public Evercrafted brand and commerce experience only. It does not contain Client SaaS workspace or Personal command content.

## Content Rules

The public site speaks with precision, material awareness, and calm confidence. It does not use discount language, countdowns, invented customer claims, ratings, testimonials, or review snippets. Product and collection details must be supplied from verified catalog data before publishing; until then, the site uses structured editorial content rather than fabricated merchandise claims.
