## How It Works

### Opening

**Heading:** A steady path through complex work.

**Body:** Evercrafted Client connects the parts of a project that usually become scattered: context, people, files, review, and delivery.

### Detailed process

**Workspace:** A member enters a workspace that is limited to the projects and records they are authorized to see.

**Project:** Each project holds its own active work, responsible people, history, and next steps.

**Studio:** Moodoor Studio gives creative assets, version context, and review work a dedicated place.

**Review:** A reviewer receives the request in context, responds with approval or changes, and leaves a record connected to the work.

**Delivery:** Delivery preparation captures the intended destination and handoff state before external publishing is enabled.
